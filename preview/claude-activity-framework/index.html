Learning
Blue is what you can read — every word the level allows you. Green is what you can use: the same measure over the words you have actually written yourself. Green always trails blue, and the gap between them is normal — recognising a word is easier than reaching for it, and that gap widens as you improve. Violet is a different question on its own scale: how many of the level's new words you have. The blue bar starts high because the commonest words carry most of the text and you already have them, so the words still worth learning move it only a little; the violet one runs the whole way. Both are estimates of how much of the next level you can handle, not of how much of its list you have ticked off — the commonest words carry most of the text, so the first few hundred matter far more than the rest — which is why the two bars move at such different speeds. Published research puts adequate comprehension at 95% and comfortable independent reading at 98%; moving up makes the next level the one you read, so the mark on the bar is 98%.
Grades what you write against the turn it answers and marks it ✓ or ✗. Tap a ✗ for the intended meaning, a better version, and which of word choice, grammar, word order or naturalness went wrong. Runs on the teaching model, about a hundredth of a cent a message. What it finds is counted under Your top mistakes below.
Graded readers introduce roughly one new word per 40–50 characters of text you already know. The partner is offered the three commonest words you have not met and uses one if it fits naturally; if none fit, the credit carries to the next turn. A new word stays highlighted and gets reused until you have seen it three times.

Sync & backup
Off by default. Sign in to back up your conversation, added words, and settings, and pick up where you left off on another device. Your OpenRouter key never syncs — it only ever stays on this device.
A private window counts as a different device. Whether sync is on, and who is signed in, live in this browser's own storage — a private or incognito window has its own, and Safari discards it when the session ends. Your cloud data is unaffected either way: turn sync on and sign in again and it all comes back. Do not rely on a private window as the only copy of anything.
Removes your conversation, added words and settings from the cloud, clears the conversation on this device, and turns cloud sync off. Words you added stay on this device. This cannot be undone.

Words by level

Grouped by what you have done with each word, commonest first inside each group — which is the order they are worth learning in. Only Not yet and Ticked as already known have boxes, because those are the two you can change: an empty box means the word is not usable yet, and ticking it says you already know it, which joins it to the allowlist at once. Words at your level or already met need no box — they are usable either way. Ticked words stay out of 词 and the flashcard exports on purpose: that list is for words you brought with you, not ones the app taught you.
