Free setup: make an account at openrouter.ai/keys, create a key, add no credit, then tick free models only below. Models priced at zero cost nothing to call. They are rate-limited and can be busy, so replies may be slower and retries more frequent; adding credit and picking a paid model fixes that.

A key is still required — there is no backend here to hold one for you, and a key shipped inside a page like this would be abused and revoked within a day. The key you paste stays in this browser's localStorage on this device, and is sent to openrouter.ai and nowhere else.
Graded readers introduce roughly one new word per 40–50 characters of text you already know. The partner is offered the three commonest words you have not met and uses one if it fits naturally; if none fit, the credit carries to the next turn. A new word stays highlighted and gets reused until you have seen it three times.
Switches the allowlist, the starters and the prompt. Words you added keep the form you added them in. About 3% of entries list several traditional variants (岸/㟁, 幫/幇/幚); the one belonging to the word's main reading is used.

Edit and it is sent as written; leave it alone and it keeps tracking the level and reply-length settings. {level} and {words} are substituted. The validator does not care what is in here — an instruction to use hard words just costs retries.
Used by the per-word Anki links in the 词 panel, which hand the card to AnkiMobile. The deck, note type and field names must match your collection exactly, or AnkiMobile rejects the card. Nested decks use ::.
Each try is another API call. One means no repairs at all: a reply that breaks the level goes straight to 我不知道。
